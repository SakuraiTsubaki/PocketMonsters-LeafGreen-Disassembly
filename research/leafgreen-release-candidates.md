# LeafGreen release candidates

The local corpus contains Japanese `BPGJ` revision 0, English `BPGE` revisions 0 and 1, and German `BPGD`, French `BPGF`, Italian `BPGI`, and Spanish `BPGS` revision 0. No Japanese revision 1 input was observed, so it is not inferred from FireRed or from the separate Decompilation project.

All seven 16 MiB inputs passed every shared GBA header check. They remain `candidate` until independently corroborated or matched by a reproducible build.

No ROM binary or byte extract is included.
