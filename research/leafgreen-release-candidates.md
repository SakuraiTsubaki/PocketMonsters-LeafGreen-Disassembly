# LeafGreen release candidates

The local corpus contains Japanese `BPGJ` revision 0 and English `BPGE` revisions 0 and 1. No Japanese revision 1 input was observed, so it is not inferred from FireRed or from the separate Decompilation project.

All three 16 MiB inputs passed every shared GBA header check. They remain `candidate` until independently corroborated or matched by a reproducible build.

No ROM binary or byte extract is included.
